# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-20)

- Hold the Emote ring key for a ring of eight emotes round the cursor; let go over one to do it.
- The eight emotes, clockwise from the top: Kneel, Pray, Bow, Wave, Nod, Thank, Curtsey, Smile.
- An emote goes to your target if you have one.
- A quick tap of the key leaves the ring open, so you can click an emote instead.
- Escape, a click outside the emotes or the key again closes the ring.
- `/emotering` or `/er` opens the ring to click.
- The key is set in Key Bindings, under AddOns, Emote ring.
- Each emote sits on a moonlit violet disc with a silver rim that glows under the cursor.
